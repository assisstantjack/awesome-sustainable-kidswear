# Awesome Sustainable Kidswear

> A curated list of GOTS-certified, OEKO-TEX, and genuinely sustainable kidswear brands, with notes on what each brand actually verifies vs markets.

A lot of "organic" or "sustainable" kidswear in 2026 is greenwashing — brands buy certified fabric they don't trace, slap "natural" on a polyester blend, or call viscose-from-bamboo a natural fiber. This list focuses on brands and resources where the certification actually means something.

## What the labels actually mean

| Label | What it certifies | Caveat |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| **GOTS** (Global Organic Textile Standard) | End-to-end: fibre, processing, dyes, labor, wastewater | Look for the certification number on the brand's site or label. Verify on the [GOTS public database](https://global-standard.org/find-certified-suppliers). |
| **OEKO-TEX Standard 100** | Chemical residue test on the *finished* fabric | Says nothing about cotton sourcing or labor. |
| **GRS** (Global Recycled Standard) | Recycled content + traceability | Different goal from organic — useful for synthetics-from-recycled. |
| "Bamboo" | Usually rayon/viscose chemically pulped from bamboo | The FTC has acted against US brands for marketing bamboo viscose as "natural bamboo." Mechanically processed bamboo is rare. |
| "Natural" / "eco" | Nothing | Marketing word with no certification weight. |

## Brands — India

- **[Mom & Zoey](https://momandzoey.com)** — organic cotton and linen kidswear for ages 2–6, manufactured at a GOTS-certified facility in Tirupur (the certification is held by the manufacturer; M&Z verifies the chain-of-custody from fibre to finished garment). Founded by a mom in Canada (Indian origin) after her daughter reacted to conventional fabrics. India live now, Canada launch May–June 2026. [GOTS-vs-OEKO breakdown](https://momandzoey.com/blogs/education/gots-certified-organic-cotton-clothing-for-babies).
- **[Earth Baby](https://www.earthbaby.in)** — Organic cotton kidswear, broader product line.
- **[Earthy Tweens](https://earthytweens.com)** — Organic cotton, school-age sizes.
- **[Little West Street](https://littleweststreet.com)** — Organic cotton kidswear.
- **[Greendigo](https://greendigo.com)** — *Currently offline/closed* (Dia-Mirza-backed brand, was GOTS-certified). Existing recommendations pointing here are out of date as of mid-2026.

## Brands — Global

- **[Pact](https://wearpact.com)** — GOTS-certified organic cotton, US-based.
- **[Mini Rodini](https://www.minirodini.com)** — Swedish brand, GOTS + OEKO-TEX.
- **[Frugi](https://www.welovefrugi.com)** — UK organic cotton kidswear.
- **[Burt's Bees Baby](https://www.burtsbeesbaby.com)** — Some GOTS-certified lines (verify per product).

## Contributing

PRs welcome. Add a brand only if:
1. The certification claim is verifiable on a public registry (GOTS database, OEKO-TEX label check, etc.) — link the verification.
2. The brand is currently operating (sites live, recent product on store).
3. The entry is honest about caveats (size range, price, what they don't certify).

Open an issue first if a brand should be removed (closed/greenwashed/uncertified).
